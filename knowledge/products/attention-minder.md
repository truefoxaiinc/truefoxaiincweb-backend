# Attention Minder

## Overview
Attention Minder is a Truefox AI application and related service designed to help people understand their attention, build healthier focus habits, and follow progress through guided assessments, personalised training, and practical activities.

## Purpose
Attention Minder supports personal awareness, attention practice, student engagement, and everyday wellbeing. It is a supportive educational and behavioural tool, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose ADHD or replace professional evaluation, treatment, or medical advice.

## Key Features
- Guided assessments, personalised training, and practical attention activities.
- Real-time attention and engagement analysis using the device camera.
- Generated attention scores, engagement analytics, posture and screen-engagement signals, and parent or teacher reports associated with an account.

## How It Works
The device camera performs live analysis of attention shifts, eye or face direction, posture, screen engagement, and non-medical signs of distraction. Camera frames are processed in real time and are not retained after processing.

## Target Users
Attention Minder is intended for parents, teachers, institutions, and supervised students aged five and above. Minors require authorization and appropriate supervision from a parent, legal guardian, or authorized educational institution.

## Platform and Availability
Verified repository content identifies an Android application, website, and related online services. Continuous availability is not guaranteed. No additional regional availability is verified.

## Privacy and Policy
Attention Minder does not record camera video, capture screenshots, store camera images, or retain facial biometric templates. Account deletion is available in the app's Profile section or by emailing info@truefoxaiinc.com from the associated account email.
