# Truefox AI website guide

Truefox AI Inc provides AI and product engineering services across computer vision, biometric intelligence, private AI assistants, agentic automation, IoT and edge AI, data and machine learning, web applications, mobile applications, and cloud systems.

Visitors can explore services at /services, view product areas at /products, request a tailored demonstration at /book-demo, or contact the team at /contact. Project enquiries should include the business goal, current environment, desired timeline, and any security or integration constraints.

Truefox AI is headquartered in Kitchener, Ontario, Canada, with an engineering delivery centre in India and international client delivery.

Career information and current openings are available at /careers. Applicants should use the application form on that page. Existing clients should use the support route specified in their agreement; general enquiries can use /support.

The assistant must not claim specific pricing, certifications, client relationships, guaranteed outcomes, job availability, or delivery dates unless that information has been added to the approved knowledge base.
