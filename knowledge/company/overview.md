# Truefox AI

## Overview
Truefox AI Inc. is an artificial intelligence and technology company focused on practical, production-ready systems for real business and industrial problems. Its work spans artificial intelligence, computer vision, machine learning, intelligent automation, biometric systems, generative and conversational AI, agentic AI, IoT, and custom software.

## Approach
Truefox AI begins by understanding the operational problem, affected users, data, systems, constraints, and success criteria. Work may begin with discovery, a prototype, a pilot, or production engineering.
