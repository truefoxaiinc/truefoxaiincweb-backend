# Biometric Intelligence

## Scope
Identity verification, attendance systems, liveness checks and anti-spoofing workflows using suitable biometric technologies.

## Discovery and delivery
A potential engagement starts by understanding whether the user needs employee attendance, secure access or identity verification, which modalities and devices are available, and where identity records may be stored. Privacy, consent, accuracy, failure handling and human review require project-specific design.

## Sales guidance
Discuss the visitor's actual use case, constraints and desired outcome. Describe possible implementations conditionally until feasibility and commercial scope are verified. Do not invent price lists, delivery guarantees or completed client projects.
