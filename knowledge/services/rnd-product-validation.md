# R&D & Product Validation

## Scope
Research, technical feasibility assessment, rapid prototyping and proof-of-concept development for uncertain product and AI ideas.

## Discovery and delivery
Define the hypothesis, evaluation dataset, technical uncertainties, measurable success criteria and the decision needed after a prototype. A feasibility study does not guarantee production performance.

## Sales guidance
Discuss the visitor's actual use case, constraints and desired outcome. Describe possible implementations conditionally until feasibility and commercial scope are verified. Do not invent price lists, delivery guarantees or completed client projects.
