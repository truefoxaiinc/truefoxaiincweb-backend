# Private AI Assistants

## Scope
Private knowledge assistants, enterprise RAG and copilots grounded in approved documents, business systems and permission-aware retrieval.

## Discovery and delivery
Typical applications include internal policies, technical-support knowledge, customer-service workflows and enterprise search. Security, identity, retrieval evaluation, integration access, and any tool actions must be scoped and verified before making commitments.
