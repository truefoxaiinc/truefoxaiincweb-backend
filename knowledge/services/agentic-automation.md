# Agentic Automation

## Scope
Governed AI agents for multi-step workflows using approved tools, APIs and enterprise systems, with oversight and approval boundaries.

## Discovery and delivery
Start by identifying the repetitive workflow, authorized systems, decisions requiring human approval, success criteria and audit requirements. Integration and degree of automation depend on approved access and implementation scope.

## Sales guidance
Discuss the visitor's actual use case, constraints and desired outcome. Describe possible implementations conditionally until feasibility and commercial scope are verified. Do not invent price lists, delivery guarantees or completed client projects.
