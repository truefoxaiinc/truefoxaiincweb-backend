# Custom AI & ML

## Scope
Custom machine-learning models, computer vision, predictive analytics, data engineering, decision systems and MLOps.

## Discovery and delivery
First establish the business outcome, availability and quality of representative data, evaluation criteria, deployment constraints and ongoing monitoring. Model accuracy or delivery timing cannot be guaranteed without assessment.

## Sales guidance
Discuss the visitor's actual use case, constraints and desired outcome. Describe possible implementations conditionally until feasibility and commercial scope are verified. Do not invent price lists, delivery guarantees or completed client projects.
