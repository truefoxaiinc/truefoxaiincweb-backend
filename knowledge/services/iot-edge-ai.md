# IoT & Edge AI

## Scope
Connected sensors, gateways, embedded software and edge inference for operational environments.

## Discovery and delivery
Explore existing devices, connectivity, latency, reliability, data retention and cloud or on-premise integration. Hardware compatibility, field deployment and real-time performance require engineering validation.

## Sales guidance
Discuss the visitor's actual use case, constraints and desired outcome. Describe possible implementations conditionally until feasibility and commercial scope are verified. Do not invent price lists, delivery guarantees or completed client projects.
