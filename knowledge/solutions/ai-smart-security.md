# AI Smart Security & CCTV Video Analytics

## What we develop
Truefox AI develops computer-vision video analytics and intelligent surveillance systems for security, safety and operational monitoring. Projects are configured to the environment, approved camera feeds, relevant events and operator response workflows.

## Camera-based use cases
Potential project capabilities include multi-camera video analysis, configurable restricted-area or perimeter events, object and activity detection, contextual alerts and event history for review. Actual feasibility and performance depend on camera placement, lighting, video quality, available interfaces and the intended use case.

## Existing camera integration
Systems can be designed around existing CCTV streams when suitable interfaces and access are available. Camera compatibility, live-stream protocol access, networking and integration constraints must be confirmed during discovery.

## Deployment and governance
Deployment options may include edge, on-premise, private cloud, cloud or hybrid processing depending on latency, bandwidth and privacy needs. Operator verification, restricted access, event logging and retention policies should be designed for the intended deployment.

## Client discovery
For prospective clients, first establish whether they already have CCTV cameras or need a new setup. Then learn which events they need to identify, who should receive alerts and whether integrations or location-specific restrictions apply. Avoid making a commitment about compatibility or detection accuracy before technical assessment.
